twixtui

TwixT in the terminal: play Alex Randolph's connection game against a bot, against someone at the same keyboard, or against someone on another machine.

● connects top to bottom, ○ connects left to right, · is an empty hole, the strokes between pegs are links and [·] is the cursor. This board is 12×12; the standard size is 24×24, and anything from 6×6 to 48×48 works.

What it does

  • A bot with three real strengths

    Beginner, intermediate and pro differ in how hard the bot actually searches, not in how they are labelled: over 60 games on a 10×10 board, pro beat intermediate 43–13. Ask for a hint on your own turn and it explains the move it would play, on the board.

  • Hotseat and remote play

    Two players at one keyboard, a direct connection between two machines, a relay you can run yourself when neither side can accept an inbound connection, or move codes pasted into chat when there is no live connection at all.

  • A tutorial you play

    Seven lessons put a real position on a real board and ask you to move into it. In the blocking lesson you have to find the single peg whose link cuts your opponent off from their border, and it tells you what went wrong when you do not.

  • Profiles, standings and honest rulesets

    Usernames with no passwords, a leaderboard of every finished game, and the three historical rulesets kept apart as explicit options: the printed box rules, the paper-and-pencil rules played online, and the original 1962 edition without swap.

Install

With Go 1.26 or newer

go install github.com/BAKocska/twixtui/cmd/twixtui@latest

Or download a binary

Static, dependency-free builds for macOS and Linux, on arm64 and x86-64, are attached to every release, together with a checksums file. 

tar xzf twixtui_<version>_darwin_arm64.tar.gz
sudo install -m 755 twixtui /usr/local/bin/twixtui
twixtui version

On macOS, a file downloaded through a browser is quarantined and Gatekeeper will refuse to run it. These binaries are not notarised, so clear the attribute yourself — or download with curl -LO, which never sets it. 

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ./twixtui

Then

  • twixtui interactive: a profile, then a menu
  • twixtui play bot --tier intermediate one game against the bot
  • twixtui play local two players, one keyboard
  • twixtui learn the interactive tutorial
