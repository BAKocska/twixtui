twixtui

TwixT in the terminal: play Alex Randolph's connection game against a bot, against someone at the same keyboard, or against someone on another machine.

● connects top to bottom, ○ connects left to right, · is an empty hole, the strokes between pegs are links, ◎ is the peg just played and [·] is the cursor. This board is 12×12; the standard size is 24×24, and anything from 6×6 to 48×48 works.

What it does

  • A bot with three search depths

    Beginner, intermediate and pro search to different depths. The test suite requires intermediate to score at least 65% against beginner over 60 games on 10×10. Pro's advantage over intermediate depends on board size, and on a small board it may not be stronger at all: 0.542 on 12×12 and 0.958 on 16×16 at the shipped budgets, and no reliable advantage anywhere from 10×10 to 16×16 once both tiers are given the same lifted guard. Pick it for the 24×24 board it was tuned on. Ask for a hint on your own turn and it explains the move it would play, on the board.

  • Hotseat and remote play

    Two players at one keyboard, a direct connection between two machines, a relay you can run yourself when neither side can accept an inbound connection, or move codes pasted into chat when there is no live connection at all.

  • A tutorial you play

    Seven lessons put a real position on a real board and ask you to move into it. In the blocking lesson you have to find the single peg whose link cuts your opponent off from their border, and it tells you what went wrong when you do not.

  • Profiles, standings and honest rulesets

    Usernames with no passwords, a leaderboard of finished games played here, and the three historical rulesets kept apart as explicit options: the printed box rules, the paper-and-pencil rules played online, and the original 1962 edition without swap. Imported records can be replayed but are not rated.

Install

With Go 1.26 or newer

go install github.com/BAKocska/twixtui/cmd/twixtui@latest

Or download a binary

Prebuilt archives for macOS and Linux, on arm64 and x86-64, are attached to each release, together with a checksums file. 

tar xzf twixtui_<version>_darwin_arm64.tar.gz
sudo install -m 755 twixtui /usr/local/bin/twixtui
twixtui version

On macOS, a file downloaded through a browser may be quarantined and refused by Gatekeeper. These binaries are not notarised, so clear the attribute yourself — or download from the command line with curl -LO, which does not set it. 

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ./twixtui

Then

  • twixtui the menu; asks who you are on the first run
  • twixtui play bot --tier intermediate --side random one game against the bot
  • twixtui play local two players, one keyboard
  • twixtui learn the interactive tutorial
